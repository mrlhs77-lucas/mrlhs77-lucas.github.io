Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026
Moodie ("the App") values your personal information and complies with applicable laws. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Account Information

Collected when you use the login feature.

  • Name (when signing in with Apple/Google)
  • Email address

User Content

Stored on our servers when you use Moodie Cloud Backup.

  • Diary text content
  • Attached photos or videos

Device and Advertising Information

  • Device ID (for ad serving purposes)
  • Crash data and diagnostic information
  • App usage behavior data (Firebase Analytics)

Purposes of Use

  • Providing Moodie Cloud backup and restore services
  • Syncing purchase history (Premium, theme stickers, etc.)
  • Improving app services and fixing errors
  • Providing personalized advertising (AdMob)
  • Responding to user inquiries

Third-Party Sharing and SDKs

Advertising

Google AdMob

Device IDs may be shared for ad serving purposes.

Analytics

Firebase Analytics

Anonymized behavioral data is collected to analyze app usage patterns.

Authentication

Google Sign-In Apple Sign-In

Used to provide login functionality.

Cloud Storage

Secure Database

Moodie Cloud backup data is encrypted and stored securely.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data is deleted along with the app when you uninstall it.
  • Cloud backup data is deleted upon account deletion request.
  • You can delete your account via Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account in the app.

Your Rights

  • You may request to view, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Ad tracking can be restricted in your device settings.
  • You may delete your account within the app at any time.

Children's Privacy

Moodie is a service intended for users of all ages. For children under 14, use under a guardian's consent is recommended, and any personal information from children is collected under the guardian's supervision.
